    1The problem

    Disease spreads in weeks. Advice comes twice a year.

    Noor farms a small plot. Her phone makes calls and sends SMS, nothing more. Too few extension officers reach her village.

    2×Extension officer visits per year, at best. Rust can cross a field in weeks.
    ABCMany farmers cannot read or write. Apps, SMS menus and leaflets leave them out.
    So we use voice. Noor calls and describes the problem in Hindi. No reading, no typing.

    6Responsible AI

    When in doubt, a person decides.

    1Checks firstReads back before advising

    It never tells her to buy or spray. Nothing is generated freely.

    Consent at registration · data never sold · audio deleted after transcription · neighbour reports only as counts per area
