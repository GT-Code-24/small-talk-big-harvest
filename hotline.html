    1Meet Noor

    Noor grows coffee.

    Her phone makes calls and sends SMS. Nothing more. When her leaves change colour, she needs an answer while it still helps.

    Phone: basic · Data: none · Language: Swahili · Plot: registered with her cooperative

    2The problem

    Disease spreads in weeks. Advice comes twice a year.

    2×Extension officer visits per year, at best. Rust can cross a plot in weeks.
    JFMAMJJASOND

    Too few extension officers. Basic phones without data. Digital tools made for English readers.

    Noor calls. From any phone.

    A free short number. No app to install, no data bundle, no reading. Just a normal phone call.

    The phone does nothing smart. All AI runs in the background system.

    One safe step. During the call.

    Symptoms plus context are ranked against a coffee disease base. The answer comes from an expert-reviewed list, plus an SMS summary.

    Leaf rust82%
    Berry disease10%
    Phoma blight8%
    SMS sent
    Answer · expert-reviewed example

    Likely coffee leaf rust.

    Collect and bury fallen leaves. Your extension officer will call you about spraying.

    07

    7Responsible AI

    When in doubt, a person decides.

    1Checks firstReads back before advising
    2Approved answersOnly expert-reviewed text
    3Not sure?A person calls back

    It never tells her to buy or spray. Nothing is generated freely.

    Consent at registration · data never sold · audio deleted after transcription · neighbour reports only as counts per area

    8Impact

    Every call is an early warning.

    Confirmed reports cluster by area and week, so rare officer visits go where the outbreak is.
